# Unlock ceremony (approved demo, 2026-11-03)

The owner approved this as the bar for Quest and Boss unlocks ("yes thats it perfect"). Open `index.html` through any local server (for example `python3 -m http.server` in this folder) with sound on.

- **Quest:** the board dims, a rumble, the camera leans in, the screen shakes, a light pillar, a flash, the node bursts out of the ground (sparks, shockwave), coins chime along the new path, then a "QUEST UNLOCKED!" fanfare.
- **Boss:** red light, a deeper hit with choir, thunder and taiko, a second shake, then "BOSS AWAKENS!".
- **Drops:** a face-down loot card shakes while its glow flickers through the tiers, then flips to Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary (random roll 60/25/12/3), each with its own reveal sound and burst.
- **Elli:** pops in and speaks. These lines are demo stand-ins; in Sunny she reacts in her own words from live context.

Sounds in `sfx/` are original, made with ElevenLabs sound generation; Elli's lines are her ElevenLabs voice. The board art is copied from `web/public/generated/adventure-board-demo/` on PR #49.

# Persona Pipeline Decision — 2026-08-09

## Decision

Keep the current practical production policy:

```text
factual child context
→ Fable authors the experience design artifact
→ Opus implements the frozen artifact
```

Do not migrate normal homework ingestion to the four-stage personality pipeline from this single experiment.

Reserve the personality-driven treatment for a controlled Quest, Boss, or unusually difficult-concept trial where its added detail may justify its reliability and cost risk. Reject the neutral staged pipeline as the current production default.

## Evidence

- Reina reviewed the three candidates blind.
- Neutral staged Candidate A scored 1/10: too wordy, unclear, and difficult to start.
- Historical Fable-to-Opus Candidate B scored 9/10: appealing colors, a discoverable hint, and a more intuitive interaction.
- Personality-driven Candidate C scored 10/10: its visible example made the task understandable and its presentation was strong; its drag interaction was clunky and still required adult orientation.
- Candidate C's first implementation exhausted its output allowance and returned truncated HTML. A larger bounded implementation completed, but the personality arm therefore had a first-pass reliability failure.
- The one evidence-driven Candidate C revision cost $1.67271 and preserved the frozen curriculum and design hashes while changing only HTML implementation.

## Interpretation

This evidence supports stronger child-facing judgment from the personality treatment in this one assignment, but it does not prove that personas alone caused the improvement. Staging, token allowance, implementation detail, and the historical benchmark are confounded.

The child review is factual experience evidence, not mastery evidence and not a universal preference claim. “Visible examples helped here” and “dragging was clunky here” may inform future hypotheses; they must not become permanent deterministic design rules.

## Production Guardrails

- Design artifacts must explicitly address first-action clarity, visible demonstration, reading load, optional help, visible world response, and falsifying evidence.
- Themes, mechanics, stakes, recovery, and interaction formats remain AI-authored.
- A personality trial must retain the same academic contract and must report first-pass validity, latency, tokens, and cost.
- A production policy changes only after repeated assignments show a material quality gain without unacceptable reliability or academic regression.
- Normal ingestion remains free of mandatory human design review after its pipeline is approved.

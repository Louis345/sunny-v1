**Notes on the polish that matters here (Rowan):**

- **The gate is mechanical, not moral.** Every station shows a locked dial (🔒) during the NAME step. Tapping it doesn't scold — it shakes, says "numbers are locked," and quietly records `unknown_named_before_computing: false`. That's dec-002 as machinery, exactly as `xd-001` specifies.
- **Errors are world events, coded distinctly.** Naming TOTAL when the total is given rains the *product* down the screen and the TOTAL bin flashes *"I never moved — 48 is 48"* (`multiply_the_given_numbers`). Naming a factor at Beat 5A's juice booth half-empties the crates (`divide_when_total_unknown`). Over/under-dialing spills or leaves leftovers; off-by-one is coded `computation_slip`. No buzzer, no red X, no scoreboard drop.
- **One gesture grammar.** Tag-tap → drop-on-hole → drop-then-dial → two cycles interleaved → dial twice against a fixed total → all of it unassisted. Nothing new to learn after Beat 3.
- **Honest gold/amber.** Assistance is one press per station, recorded as `prompted` / `modeled` / `prompted_keep_going`, and the arc colour follows the record, never the outcome. The Beat 6 keep-going nudge only fires after 18s of stall, so `second_pair_unprompted` stays truthful.
- **The finale is the child's own answer.** The sky array is built from a factor pair *they* found for 24, with gold/amber lanterns in the proportion actually earned.
- No storage, no Sunny APIs, no speech synthesis, no oscillators; every sound cue was replaced by a visual twin, so the experience is fully legible muted.
- Inherited honesty, unchanged: this packet embeds naming in every item, so nothing here can separate naming from practice. The NAME-step latency is logged per beat and surfaced on the closing plaque so the naming-adds-cost check is at least measurable.

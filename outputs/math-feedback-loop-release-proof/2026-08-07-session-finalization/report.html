Provider cost for this proof

  • Skip records rating unavailable; no neutral score is invented.
  • Ratings remain engagement evidence and cannot become mastery.
  • Concurrent retries are coalesced through an owned, heartbeating cross-process lease.
  • All canonical cycle transitions use a short atomic lock, preventing cross-process lost updates.
  • Transition locks are owner-scoped; brief evidence-write contention retries the saved Planner result rather than buying another call.
  • A crashed process can be recovered after lease expiry; its old owner ID cannot commit or fail the replacement decision.
  • Delayed earlier-node modal evidence is preserved without repeating the paid Planner call.
  • Locked, never-launched nodes cannot submit abandonment evidence.
  • A provider failure leaves the finalized packet available for a later retry.
  • Repository, route, runtime, and closed-loop acceptance checks use temporary child roots; Reina and Ila are untouched.

Files permitted to change in this task

The canonical assignment cycle may gain one session finalization, one engagement evidence reference, and—after the provider succeeds—one theory decision. Raw choice events remain immutable. No profile, mastery, or child data is changed by this proof run.

Skyharbor Multiplication Run

The board unifies all six baseline nodes under a single Skyharbor world so the two mechanic routes (speed vs. puzzle) read as two harbor duties rather than two disconnected games, honoring the immutable route/experiment structure while giving Reina one coherent place. Design choices are grounded directly in her evidence: the speed route uses rapid, stationary-target tap-select (gliders, crate dash, delivery drops) mirroring her strongly positive speed dimension (5.0, conf .875) and her clean, low-frustration completion of skybird-delivery-run; the puzzle route uses tap-to-snap block/vault/crane mechanics that honor her highest-confidence puzzle preference (conf .92) while deliberately avoiding the drag interaction that caused her 0.75 frustration and abandonment on gearworks-crate-builder. Story is present but kept light (banners, crane/glider framing) matching her only mildly positive, lower-confidence story weight, so it seasons rather than dominates every node. Competition, control-heavy, and calm framings are avoided per her negative/mixed-negative weights. Every node's first visual moment (a pulsing platform, glowing tile, or brighter block) demonstrates the tap target through motion and light alone, so the first meaningful action never depends on adult explanation. Mathematical difficulty increases in structure (grounding to retrieval to transfer) while the interface stays constant within each route, per the mandate that difficulty may rise without interface decoding rising. The quest and boss teasers extend the same sky-harbor geography (tower, beacon) so the locked nodes feel like a visible destination rather than a bolted-on reward screen.

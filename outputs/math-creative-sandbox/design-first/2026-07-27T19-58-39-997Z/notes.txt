Design-first experiment result

- One model call.
- Model: claude-sonnet-5.
- Generation time: 349,327 ms.
- The model chose GSAP for animation and Tone.js for sound.
- The opening screen expresses the approved mission, stakes, progression, and mathematical role substantially better than the unconstrained one-shot activity.
- Generation reached the 32,000-token limit and stopped in the middle of the validation function.

Conclusion

The design-first artifact materially improved creative direction and gave the model a reason to use richer libraries. The experiment also exposed a delivery bottleneck: asking one call to implement a richer experience exceeded the output budget.

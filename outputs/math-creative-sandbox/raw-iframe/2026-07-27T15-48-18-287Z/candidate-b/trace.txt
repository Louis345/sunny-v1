What I clicked or entered
- Started the bakery, made an intentionally incorrect basket check, configured 4 baskets of 5 muffins, built a 5 by 3 tray, and answered four bakery word problems.

What visibly changed
- Basket and muffin counts changed; skip-count totals and equations updated; additional sections unlocked; the tray array changed; progress dots advanced; small bakery icons accumulated; a completion summary appeared.

Whether random clicking advanced the experience
- Plus and minus controls could be clicked until they matched the explicitly stated target. Multiple-choice story problems could be guessed. Incorrect answers did not create a substantial world reaction.

Whether I wanted to continue
- It was coherent and slightly more unified than Candidate A, but still felt like a reskinned lesson rather than an experience with meaningful anticipation or stakes.

Scenario D result

- Generation completed in 122,990 ms with one model call.
- The model chose plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript and did not use a third-party library despite having permission.
- The opening action is understandable.
- Equal groups and word problems use working interactive steppers.
- Mathematical changes are visible and correct.
- Progress is represented by stars and solved counts.
- The honey-market theme is mostly presentation around a conventional multi-section lesson.
- The world does not materially evolve as the child succeeds.
- The final feedback is a small star increase and confirmation message, not a strong payoff.
- Allowing libraries alone did not produce a significant creative or experiential improvement.

Conclusion

The library restriction was not the immediate bottleneck in this run: the model voluntarily chose not to use one. The remaining bottleneck is experience conception—turning mathematical reasoning into an evolving purpose—rather than access to Three.js by itself.

Welcome to Stamp Quest. Six shapes are lining up for the magic stamp pad.

Watch each shape spin. If it has a flat side, it can press down and leave a print.

Here comes the test. Flat sides touch the pad like a stamp.

Quick pick. One shape is all curve, and it is going to roll away. Which one is it?

The prints are in. Cube makes square. Prism makes rectangle. Cylinder and cone make circles. Pyramid makes triangle.

The sphere cannot press down flat, so it rolls off with no print. Flat makes a face. Curved makes a roll.

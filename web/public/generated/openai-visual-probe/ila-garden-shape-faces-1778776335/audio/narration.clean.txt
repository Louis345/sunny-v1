Welcome to Ila's breakfast print garden. Watch the shapes get ready to make prints.

A flat face works like the bottom of a stamp. When it presses down, it can leave a flat picture.

The toast cube can stamp a square. The pancake cylinder can stamp a circle. Now look at the last shape: which one has no flat side to stamp?

Look at the blueberry sphere. It is all curve, so it cannot press a flat face into the syrup. It rolls away and leaves no flat print.

Tiny rule locked in: flat faces can print. Curved surfaces roll.

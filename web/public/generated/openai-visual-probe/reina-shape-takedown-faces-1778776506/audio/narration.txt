Welcome to Reina's face print arena. Watch the shapes enter the mat.

Your first move is to scout for a flat face. A flat face can pin the mat and leave a two dimensional print.

The cube pins a square. The cylinder pins a circle. The cone can pin a circle with its flat base. Now choose: which solid has no flat face to pin the mat?

Look at the sphere. It is all curve, so it cannot pin the mat. It rolls away and leaves no flat print.

Champion rule locked in: flat faces make prints. Curves roll away.

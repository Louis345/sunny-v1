# Sunny Creation Studio — Concept Hold

**Status:** Early product idea. Preserve in the wardrobe sandbox, refine with the design, and do not merge into the active product until the experience and safety boundaries are approved.

## Vision

Sunny can turn earned currency into meaningful, creative experiences with Elli. Instead of coins existing only to buy companion clothing, a child could spend them on activities such as drawing together, making a superhero video, or watching something they previously created.

This should feel like entering a creative space with the companion—not opening a generic generation form. Elli should collaborate from live context, express preferences, react to choices, and remain aware of what the child is making.

## Possible Experience

1. The child completes real baseline or learning activities and earns currency.
2. From FaceTime or the companion world, the child enters the **Creation Studio**.
3. Elli offers a few child-appropriate possibilities based on current interests and previously measured preferences.
4. The child and Elli build the idea together on a dedicated canvas—for example, choosing a hero, powers, setting, tone, and visual style.
5. Sunny shows the exact coin cost before any generation begins. Elli may have an opinion, but cannot spend the child's currency.
6. An approved media provider could generate the artifact. Grok is one possible provider, not a required product dependency.
7. Elli reacts to the finished creation and can discuss, narrate, or watch it with the child.
8. The child can save the result to a personal **Library** for later viewing or continuation.

## Product Relationship

The intended loop is:

`learning effort → earned currency → companion shopping or creative experience → saved ownership and memories`

The store, closet, Creation Studio, and Library should share one wallet and one ownership ledger. Clothing belongs to the companion inventory; generated media belongs to the child's library.

The Creation Studio must be a new intentional product surface. It must not revive or depend on the retired legacy learning-session canvas.

## Core Boundaries

- Currency must come from recorded, legitimate activity completion—not invented progress.
- The child owns the wallet and confirms spending. Elli can want, recommend, celebrate, or be disappointed, but cannot pressure or transact.
- Generation costs, failures, refunds, and saved ownership must be explicit and auditable.
- Prompts and outputs need child-safety review appropriate to images, audio, and video.
- Provider-specific code should sit behind a media-generation contract so Grok or another service can be changed later.
- Generated artifacts need provenance: child, prompt/brief, provider, cost, creation time, safety result, and library status.
- Creative rewards must not make educational claims or become evidence of mastery.
- Parent controls should determine which generation types, sharing options, and spending limits are available.

## Design Questions To Resolve

- Is the studio entered from FaceTime, the virtual store/world, or both?
- Is creation primarily conversation-led, canvas-led, or a blend?
- Which first artifact proves the concept: drawing, comic panel, short narrated scene, or superhero video?
- How are coin prices set, and what happens when generation fails?
- Can Elli appear inside the generated story, and who approves that representation?
- What is saved locally versus remotely, and how does a parent delete an artifact?
- Does the Library support replay only, or also sequels, edits, and remixing?

## Merge Gate

Do not merge this concept into the active Sunny experience until we have:

- an approved entry point and screen flow;
- a clear wallet, purchase, failure, and refund contract;
- a child-safe generation and moderation plan;
- a Library ownership and deletion model;
- one deliberately scoped first media type;
- confirmation that the learning evidence loop remains separate from creative reward activity.

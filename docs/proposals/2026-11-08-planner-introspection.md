# Planner introspection (approved to build, owner 2026-11-08)

## The problem

Each board, the Planner writes a theory and predictions, and code scores them. It never gets back, in one place, **its own track record**: what it tried for which error, what it expected, and what actually happened. Ila wrote "inport" again and again; an explainer and radar practice did not fix it; the next board re-taught the same way. A tutor who reads their own notes would stop and try something new, such as building a game aimed at m-before-p. The goal is the child passing the test: every board should be a better try than the last, for a reason you can read.

## What changes

1. **Her track record, per stuck word (facts code computes).** For each word on the list: every answer with the board, the activity, help or no help, and what she wrote ("import: on her own 0 of 4, wrote inport x3, impot x1; tried: picture explainer (board 3), radar practice (boards 3, 4); latest on her own: inport"). Words she has secured are listed as secured.
2. **Its own journal (its words, fed back).** For each earlier board: what it said it would change and test, the stops it built, and the result for the words it targeted. Kept in her chart; the next board's Planner reads it first.
3. **A self-review before every board (written by the Planner).** The decision tool gains `self_review`: what I tried, what happened, why I think it happened, what I do differently now. It plans from that review. Nothing tells it what to conclude.
4. **What its tools can really do.** The built-for-her game can be aimed at one error ("focus": m-before-p); the focus goes to the game builder. How she responds to each kind of game (finished, replayed, chosen) sits beside the results.
5. **You can read it.** Each self-review and journal entry is written to `homework/planner-journal/<homework>.md` in her folder and logged as ` 🎮 [planner-journal] [review] ...`.

## Not changing

No rules about what to choose, no thresholds, no do-not lines. Record-keeping stays honest (help is help; practice is not a check). Math gets the same journal later on the same pipeline.

## Contract

LEARNING_FEEDBACK_LOOP.md gains a section, "The Planner reviews itself" (contract 56): before each decision the Planner reads its journal and her track record and writes its self-review; the review is stored with the decision.

## How we will know

On Ila's real history (a copy, no paid call needed to build), the next-board request shows her track record for import, dormant, ornament and pardon, and its journal. Her next real board's self-review is readable, and we see whether it changes approach on words its tools have not fixed.
